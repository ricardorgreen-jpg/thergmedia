Urban Cinematics

The Vision

Capturing the raw energy of the Chicago skyline through 4K drone cinematography and high-speed ground tracking. This project focused on the intersection of architecture and movement.

We utilized a minimalist editing style to let the natural light and urban textures drive the narrative.
